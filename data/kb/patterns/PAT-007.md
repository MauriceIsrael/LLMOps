# PAT-007 — Advisory assistant with prepare-only tooling

## Problem

A reasoning assistant would materially help diagnosis, but giving it the ability to act turns a probabilistic component into an operational risk and blurs accountability.

## Forces

- Operators benefit most from explanation and preparation, not execution.
- Model errors are confident and plausible.
- Audit must remain unambiguous about who decided.

## Solution

Expose read operations only, plus at most a prepare operation returning a pre-filled workflow reference that a human then launches and another approves. Keep the assistant off the critical path so it can be disabled without operational consequence. Version agent definitions and prompts like any other rule.

## Trade-offs

The assistant cannot close the loop, which is the point. Value is measured in diagnosis time rather than in actions taken.

## When not to use this

In non-production environments with no client data and no service impact, write-capable tooling is legitimate and considerably more useful.

## Seen in

One mission-critical mobile network engagement (2026), across transport, container platform and vendor-managed core.
