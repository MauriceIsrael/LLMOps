# PAT-004 — Break-glass with an independent failure domain

## Problem

The tooling built to restore service is unavailable during the incident it was built for, because it shares infrastructure with what failed.

## Forces

- Automation concentrates capability, therefore concentrates risk.
- Operators lose the manual skill the automation replaced.
- The failure is discovered exactly when it cannot be fixed.

## Solution

Run the chain on a dedicated cluster with its own storage and its own southbound paths. Export the critical recovery configurations, in executable form, to a location reachable without the platform, refreshed by the same pipeline that validates them. Train at least two operators and drill the procedure periodically.

## Trade-offs

Additional footprint and a procedure to maintain. The drill costs a half-day per period and is the only thing that proves the arrangement works.

## When not to use this

For systems where a prolonged outage is commercially unpleasant but not dangerous, the drill cadence can be relaxed — but the independence of the failure domain should not be.

## Seen in

One mission-critical mobile network engagement (2026), across transport, container platform and vendor-managed core.
