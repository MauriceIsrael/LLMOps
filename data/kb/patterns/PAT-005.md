# PAT-005 — Two observation planes, one collection layer

## Problem

Service assurance and platform operations need different tools, different retention and different audiences, but instrumenting every source twice is wasteful and produces divergent truths.

## Forces

- Audit retention on service signals is long; platform retention is operational.
- Platform teams and network operations teams want different products.
- Correlation must not be fragmented by the split.

## Solution

Collect once. Route at the gateway on signal attributes towards the two backends. Forward health and alert events from the infrastructure plane into the service plane so that correlation and closed-loop triggering remain performed by a single engine. Adding or replacing a backend becomes a gateway configuration change.

## Trade-offs

Two backends to operate and licence. Requires discipline in attribute tagging at the source, otherwise routing degrades into duplication.

## When not to use this

Where one team operates both service and platform, and retention needs are similar, a single backend with index and role separation is simpler and should be preferred.

## Seen in

One mission-critical mobile network engagement (2026), across transport, container platform and vendor-managed core.
