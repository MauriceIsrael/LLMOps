# PAT-002 — Shadow validation of rules

## Problem

A detection rule cannot be trusted until it has met real traffic, but arming it in production to find out is exactly the risk being avoided.

## Forces

- Pre-production traffic never reproduces real noise, bursts or edge cases.
- Rule quality is measurable, but only against reality.
- A validation environment fed with production data is itself a risk if it can act.

## Solution

Two layers. Batch: replay stored alarm history against the candidate rule to measure coverage of known incidents. Real-time: deploy the rule in an isolated environment fed by a temporary, filtered and pseudonymized duplicate of production traffic, where the rule can only record what it would have triggered. Promote by reviewed change against quantified exit criteria.

## Trade-offs

Requires an isolated environment and a duplication path. Delays arming by the length of the observation window. In exchange, false-positive rates are known before anyone is woken up by them.

## When not to use this

For rules whose only effect is to raise a low-priority ticket, the cost of the apparatus exceeds the risk. Deploy those directly in report-only mode instead.

## Seen in

One mission-critical mobile network engagement (2026), across transport, container platform and vendor-managed core.
