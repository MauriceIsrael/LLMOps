# PPDR-DEVICE-ATEX — ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU Explosion-Proof Equipment Certification (Zones 1/21)

## Specification Reference
Directive 2014/34/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on equipment intended for use in potentially explosive atmospheres (ATEX) and EN IEC 60079 (Explosive atmospheres - Part 11: Intrinsic safety "i").

## Technical Requirement
Handheld terminals, headsets, and remote speaker microphones (RSMs) allocated to hazardous industrial operations, chemical facilities, or firefighting brigades must hold formal ATEX certification for explosive gas (Zone 1) and combustible dust (Zone 21) environments, fulfilling intrinsic safety classifications II 2G Ex ib IIC T4 Gb and II 2D Ex ib IIIC T135°C Db.

## Architecture Acceptance Criteria
- Valid EU-Type Examination Certificate issued by an accredited European Notified Body.
- Mechanically secured battery compartment with tamper-resistant latch requiring specialized tooling.
- Electronic current/voltage limiting circuitry preventing spark generation during physical impact or component rupture.
