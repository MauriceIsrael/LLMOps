# PPDR-DEVICE-RUGGED — Tactical Ruggedization: MIL-STD-810H & IP68/IP69K Ingress Protection

## Specification Reference
Department of Defense MIL-STD-810H (Environmental Engineering Considerations and Laboratory Tests) and IEC 60529 / ISO 20653 (Degrees of protection provided by enclosures).

## Technical Requirement
Handheld field terminals, accessories, and portable equipment deployed with emergency first responders must be physically hardened to endure extreme tactical handling, certified against MIL-STD-810H environmental tests and achieving minimum IP68 (continuous water immersion) and IP69K (high-pressure washdown) ratings.

## Architecture Acceptance Criteria
- Certified survival of repeated drops from 1.8 meters onto concrete surfaces across all faces, edges, and corners.
- Operational temperature range covering -30°C to +60°C continuous operation without thermal throttling of RF power.
- Glove-operable and wet-finger-operable high-brightness touch displays (> 800 nits daylight readability).
- Robust mechanical docking connectors resistant to salt fog and corrosive chemicals.
