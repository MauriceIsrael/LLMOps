# ISO-27001-A8-24 — Use of Cryptography and Key Management Policy

## Regulatory Reference
ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A, Control A.8.24 (Use of cryptography).

## Standard Requirement
Rules for the effective use of cryptography, including cryptographic key management, shall be defined and implemented.

## Architecture Acceptance Criteria
- Comprehensive end-to-end encryption policy for all communication traversing shared or untrusted networks.
- Documented key lifecycle: secure pseudo-random generation, protected distribution, automated rotation, and revocation.
- Deprecation of legacy algorithms (strict ban on DES, 3DES, MD5, SHA-1; enforcement of AES-256-GCM and Ed25519/ECDSA P-384).
