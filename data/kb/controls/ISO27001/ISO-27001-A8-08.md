# ISO-27001-A8-08 — Management of Technical Vulnerabilities

## Regulatory Reference
ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A, Control A.8.8 (Management of technical vulnerabilities).

## Standard Requirement
Information about technical vulnerabilities of information systems being used shall be obtained, exposure evaluated, and appropriate measures taken.

## Architecture Acceptance Criteria
- Automated container image vulnerability scanning in CI/CD pipelines with automated deployment rejection for critical unpatched CVEs.
- Emergency patch management procedure with SLA < 48 hours for vulnerabilities of CVSS severity >= 9.0.
- Periodic audit of open-source components and transitive dependencies.
