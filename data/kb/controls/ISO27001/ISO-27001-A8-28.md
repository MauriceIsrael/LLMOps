# ISO-27001-A8-28 — Protection of Logging Records and Integrity Assurance

## Regulatory Reference
ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A, Control A.8.28 (Protection of log information).

## Standard Requirement
Log information shall be protected against tampering and unauthorized access.

## Architecture Acceptance Criteria
- Audit log storage on Write Once Read Many (WORM) media or with time-stamped cryptographic sealing.
- Standard systems administrators possess zero edit or delete rights over security audit logs.
- Retention compliant with regulatory requirements (minimum 1 year history accessible).
