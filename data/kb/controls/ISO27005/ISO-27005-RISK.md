# ISO-27005-RISK — Information Security Risk Management (ISO 27005 / MONARC / EBIOS RM)

## Specification Reference
ISO/IEC 27005:2022 (Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection - Guidance on managing information security risks) and competent national risk assessment methods (e.g. MONARC in Luxembourg, EBIOS RM in France, BSI Standard 200-2 in Germany).

## Technical Requirement
The system architecture and end-to-end operational processes must undergo formal security risk assessment in compliance with ISO/IEC 27005, adopting recognized national methodologies (such as MONARC) to identify threat actors, evaluate business impacts, establish residual risk acceptance, and continuously update risk treatment plans.

## Architecture Acceptance Criteria
- Full risk assessment deliverable detailing asset valuation, threat scenarios, likelihood, and impact matrices.
- Formal mapping between identified risk vectors and selected security controls (ISO 27001, NIS2, SecNumCloud).
- Risk assessment re-evaluation triggered upon major architectural release, critical CVE discovery, or at least annually.
