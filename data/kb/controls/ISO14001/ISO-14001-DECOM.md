# ISO-14001-DECOM — Sustainable Decommissioning, Low Carbon Footprint & Circular Asset Recycling

## Specification Reference
ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental management systems - Requirements with guidance for use) and Directive 2012/19/EU on waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE).

## Technical Requirement
All hardware decommissioning plans must comply with ISO 14001 principles, enforcing minimized carbon footprints during transport, prioritized equipment reuse or component refurbishment, compliant e-waste handling, certified cryptographic destruction of sensitive media, and certified hazardous material recycling.

## Architecture Acceptance Criteria
- Formal End-of-Life (EoL) and Decommissioning Plan submitted prior to system commissioning.
- Certified cryptographic sanitization and physical destruction (DIN 66399 level H-5/E-5) for storage drives and HSMs.
- Documented traceability of recycled materials and WEEE compliance certificates for all retired telecom assets.
