# NIS2-ART23-2 — Informing service recipients of significant cyber threats

## Regulatory Reference
Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS 2), Article 23(2).

## Legal Requirement
Where applicable, Member States shall ensure that essential and important entities communicate, without undue delay, to the recipients of their services that are potentially affected by a significant cyber threat any measures or remedies that those recipients are able to take in response to that threat. Where appropriate, the entities shall also inform those recipients of the significant cyber threat itself.

## Architecture Acceptance Criteria
- The architecture includes a channel to inform the recipients of the services potentially affected by a significant cyber threat of the measures or remedies they can take.
- The service catalogue identifies, for each service, the recipients to inform and the communication means that remain available during a crisis.
