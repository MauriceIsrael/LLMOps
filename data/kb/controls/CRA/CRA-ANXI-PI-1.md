# CRA-ANXI-PI-1 — ESSENTIAL CYBERSECURITY REQUIREMENTS — Products with digital elements shall be designed, developed and produced in such a way…

## Regulatory Reference
Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act), Annex I, Part I, point (1).

## Legal Requirement
Products with digital elements shall be designed, developed and produced in such a way that they ensure an appropriate level of cybersecurity based on the risks.
