# ADR-0003 — Orchestration and approval engine

## Context

Operators are not developers. They need buttons, forms and role-scoped access, and every production action must carry a recorded human approval.

## Options considered

**Command-line playbooks only.** No cost; unusable by first-line operators, no approval trail.

**Lightweight job runner.** Simple to operate, gives buttons and forms; no native approval node, weaker role model.

**Full orchestration platform with workflow approval nodes.** Buttons, surveys, fine-grained roles, approval nodes with timeout and notification, API-drivable; heavier to operate.

## Decision

A full orchestration platform is used, with workflow approval nodes as the mandatory gate for production actions.

## Consequences

Approval, notification and audit are obtained natively rather than built. Role modelling becomes an explicit design task. The platform is one more component to operate on the management cluster.

## Revisit when

The service management platform's own approval workflow can gate execution directly without losing the operator-facing buttons.
