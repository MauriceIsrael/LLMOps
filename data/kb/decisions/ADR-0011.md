# ADR-0011 — Inference served locally on general-purpose processors

## Context

Re-examination of ADR-0010 concluded that sending configuration fragments, addresses and potentially credential material to an external provider is not defensible for an accredited mission-critical system, even with redaction controls.

## Options considered

**Keep the external endpoint with stronger controls.** Best latency; the control remains procedural and must be re-proven at every audit.

**Local inference on general-purpose processors with a small model.** Structural confidentiality, compatible with a disconnected deployment; response times of tens of seconds to minutes, and reduced tool-calling reliability with smaller models.

**Defer until acceleration hardware is funded.** Zero risk, zero benefit.

## Decision

Inference is served locally on a dedicated node pool, starting on general-purpose processors with a small model. The endpoint is a configuration value so that acceleration hardware can be added later without redesign.

## Consequences

Confidentiality becomes structural rather than contractual. Capacity replaces confidentiality as the limiting factor, so latency and answer quality must be measured before opening the production profile. Read-only tooling keeps model errors informational rather than operational.

## Revisit when

Acceleration hardware becomes available, or measurement shows latency is unacceptable even for engineering use.
