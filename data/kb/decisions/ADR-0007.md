# ADR-0007 — Dedicated management cluster with its own storage and southbound paths

## Context

The automation chain, the analytics and the collection tier must run somewhere. The simplest option is to place them on an existing workload cluster.

## Options considered

**Co-hosting on a workload cluster.** Cheapest; the recovery tooling shares a failure domain with what it recovers.

**Dedicated management cluster with its own storage and its own paths to the element manager and the network devices.** Additional footprint; removes the circular dependency and allows an independent break-glass path.

## Decision

A dedicated management cluster is used, and a documented, drilled break-glass procedure exists for its own unavailability.

## Consequences

Additional cluster footprint and lifecycle. In exchange, an incident on the workload clusters cannot disable the chain that repairs them. The break-glass export becomes a deliverable to maintain and to exercise.

## Revisit when

The platform offers a supported control-plane isolation model that provides the same guarantee at lower footprint.
