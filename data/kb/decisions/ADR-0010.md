# ADR-0010 — Inference served by an external endpoint

## Context

No local acceleration hardware was available. The assistant layer needs a reasoning model, and the vendor supports external providers.

## Options considered

**External provider with contractual and technical controls.** No hardware investment, best latency and quality; tool outputs may carry configuration fragments, addresses and credentials outside the trust boundary.

**Defer the assistant layer.** No exposure; loses the benefit.

**Local inference on general-purpose processors.** Keeps everything inside the boundary; degraded latency.

## Decision

Use an external inference endpoint with scope restriction, redaction and contracted data-protection terms.

## Consequences

Confidentiality becomes a procedural control that must be proven repeatedly to an accreditation authority. Superseded shortly after being taken, once the security trade-off was re-examined.

## Revisit when

Superseded — see ADR-0011.
