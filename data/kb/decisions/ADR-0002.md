# ADR-0002 — Network source of truth and compliance engine

## Context

An inventory of record is needed to drive automation, feed the configuration database, and support drift and compliance reporting across three domains.

## Options considered

**Commercial orchestrator suite.** Strong native modelling, transactional configuration; licence cost and lock-in on the state.

**Inventory product with a configuration compliance extension.** Open source, active ecosystem, native backup / intended / compliance workflow, extensible jobs and buttons; requires assembling the transport layer.

**Spreadsheet plus scripts.** Zero licence, unbounded maintenance cost, no compliance story.

## Decision

An open-source inventory product extended with a configuration compliance module is the network source of truth and the drift and compliance engine.

## Consequences

The compliance workflow is obtained rather than built. Population of the inventory becomes the critical path item of the foundation work package, and is routinely underestimated by a factor of two.

## Revisit when

Inventory ownership moves to the service management platform's network inventory product and can drive automation directly.
