# ADR-0008 — Split observability: service plane and infrastructure plane

## Context

Two capable products are in the landscape: a search and analytics platform already used for service signals, and the container platform vendor's topology-centric observability product. Both ingest open telemetry and both can alert.

## Options considered

**Single analytics platform for everything.** One product to operate and licence; loses topology and health-model experience for the platform, and the vendor's platform observability extensions.

**Single platform observability product for everything.** Loses long audit retention, free-text investigation, field-level security and the replay capability the shadow validation depends on.

**Both, split by nature of signal.** Each product does what it is best at; requires an explicit boundary and a bridge to keep correlation single-engine.

## Decision

Signals contributing to a delivered service go to the analytics platform; signals describing the hosting platform go to the vendor observability product. A single collection layer routes on signal attributes, and the infrastructure plane forwards health events into the service plane so that correlation and closed-loop triggering remain performed by one engine.

## Consequences

Two products to operate and licence. Clear audience separation. No source is instrumented twice. The bridge between planes becomes a design item that must not be forgotten, or the split fragments diagnosis.

## Revisit when

The platform observability product is not in the contractual scope, in which case a single analytics platform with index and role separation is preferred.
