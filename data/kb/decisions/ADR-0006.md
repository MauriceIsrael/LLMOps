# ADR-0006 — GitOps engine for the container platform

## Context

The container platform hosts the mission-critical application suite and the other services. A continuous reconciliation engine is required, and the platform vendor ships one.

## Options considered

**Application-centric GitOps engine.** Excellent application-oriented user experience, large community; a second control plane alongside the platform's own.

**The platform's native multi-cluster GitOps engine.** One control plane, aligned with platform support and lifecycle, well suited to a fleet of clusters; less application-centric tooling.

## Decision

The platform's native GitOps engine is used, and it is the only reconciliation engine for that domain.

## Consequences

One control plane and one support path. Workload drift is detected natively. Cluster definitions and node operating system configuration remain outside its scope and must be covered by the automation chain — a distinct engineering item that is easy to overlook.

## Revisit when

The team standardizes on the application-centric engine for other reasons; running both remains excluded.
