# ADR-0009 — Mastership arbitration between inventory systems

## Context

Three systems legitimately claim to hold inventory: the network source of truth driving automation, the service management platform's network inventory, and the configuration management database.

## Options considered

**Network inventory product as master, automation reading from it.** Aligns with the service management strategy; unproven as a driver for automation inventory, and dependent on release capability.

**Network source of truth as master for automation-facing data, one-way sync outward.** Immediately workable; leaves the service model to the inventory product.

**Bidirectional synchronization.** Superficially attractive, produces irreconcilable records.

## Decision

For the first phase, the network source of truth masters device and cluster data used by automation; the network inventory product masters the service and resource model; the configuration management database masters configuration items. Synchronization is one-directional per attribute.

## Consequences

No circular synchronization. The arbitration must be validated with the platform owner during framing, and is expected to be revisited once the inventory product is in service. This is currently the least verified decision in the set.

## Revisit when

The network inventory product demonstrates it can drive automation inventory directly at the required fidelity.
