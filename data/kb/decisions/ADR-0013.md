# ADR-0013 — Subscriber and SIM provisioning excluded from the configuration chain

## Context

The service management platform's network inventory can model subscriber identity modules as resources, which raised the question of whether their lifecycle belongs in the configuration management perimeter.

## Options considered

**Include provisioning in the configuration chain.** One chain for everything; mixes a subscriber data flow with a system configuration flow, with different controls, different audit needs and different failure modes.

**Keep provisioning as a separate flow.** Clear separation of concerns; requires an explicit integration to specify and budget.

## Decision

Inventory and ordering workflow stay with the service management platform; profile generation, download and activation stay with the certified provisioning platform and the subscriber database. The configuration chain does not participate.

## Consequences

The integration between the ordering workflow and the provisioning platform must be specified and costed rather than assumed available. Out-of-the-box coverage claims must be verified against the exact purchased release.

## Revisit when

A vendor demonstrates an integrated capability covering both, with controls acceptable for subscriber data.
