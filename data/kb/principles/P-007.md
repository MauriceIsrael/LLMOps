# P-007 — Respect the vendor boundary

## Statement

Where a vendor element manager owns the configuration of its domain, automation orchestrates above it through official northbound interfaces, never in its place, and deploys nothing on the vendor platform.

## Why

Bypassing an element manager creates two configuration masters, triggers unwanted resynchronizations and voids supportability. The apparent shortcut costs more than the interface it avoids.

## Implications

- Backup becomes an export, drift becomes a comparison of exports, a fallback plan becomes a pre-validated delta.
- Observability of that domain is limited to what the vendor exports; the gap is declared rather than discovered.

## How to verify

Check that no credential exists for direct node access, and that no third-party component is installed on the vendor platform.
