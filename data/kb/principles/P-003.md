# P-003 — Bounded unitary actions

## Statement

Closed loops execute simple, idempotent playbooks acting on a single asset, with a known blast radius, pre-checks and post-checks. On failure they stop cleanly and escalate; they never attempt compensation.

## Why

Multi-domain orchestration with error compensation is an order of magnitude more complex to build and to certify than unitary action, and its failure modes are the ones nobody rehearses. A playbook that fails cleanly and escalates is worth more than one that tries to be clever.

## Implications

- No cross-asset transactions in the closed loop.
- Error handling is a two-branch tree: success then post-check, or stop and escalate.
- Complex recovery remains a human-driven runbook, possibly assisted.

## How to verify

Review each remediation playbook: it must touch one asset, be re-runnable without side effects, and have no recovery branch.
