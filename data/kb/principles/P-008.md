# P-008 — The chain observes itself

## Statement

Executions, configuration differences, compliance results and approval decisions are collected, indexed and rendered like any other operational signal.

## Why

An automation chain that cannot be observed cannot be trusted, and cannot produce the evidence an audit requires.

## Implications

- Automation outputs are a first-class signal source.
- Dashboards cover drift, compliance, executions and decisions, not only the managed system.

## How to verify

Produce, for any past week, the list of executions with their outcome and approver from the observability plane alone.
