# P-010 — One master per data domain

## Statement

Each data domain has exactly one writing system; every other system holds a read-only copy. Synchronization is one-directional per attribute, not per system.

## Why

Two products both claiming to be the source of truth produce circular synchronizations and irreconcilable records, and the reconciliation cost is paid forever.

## Implications

- The arbitration is explicit, recorded, and revisited when a product's role changes.
- Where a product offers overlapping capability, the boundary is agreed with the vendor before integration starts.

## How to verify

For each shared attribute, name the single writer. An attribute with two writers is a defect.
