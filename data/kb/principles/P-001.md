# P-001 — Generalized GitOps

## Statement

Intended configurations, fallback plans, triggering rules, correlation rules and automation code are versioned in Git. Every change goes through review and a validation pipeline before it can reach a managed system.

## Why

A managed system with more than one entry path cannot be reasoned about. Versioning is not primarily about rollback; it is about making every change reviewable, attributable and reproducible, which is what an accreditation authority asks for.

## Implications

- The pipeline, not an operator, is the only writer.
- Rollback becomes a revert plus a redeploy rather than a separate procedure.
- Anything not in Git does not exist: undocumented manual changes appear as drift, which is the intended behaviour.

## How to verify

Attempt a manual change on a managed asset outside the pipeline; it must surface in the next drift report.
