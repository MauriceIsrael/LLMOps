# P-004 — Graduated autonomy

## Statement

Moving an action from supervised to automatic approval is a decision taken per class of event, on measured evidence gathered in shadow mode and during a probation period. It is never a design default.

## Why

Autonomy is earned by evidence, not granted by architecture. Tying it to measured false-positive rates makes the trajectory defensible to an accreditation authority and reversible if behaviour degrades.

## Implications

- Each event class carries its autonomy level and the evidence that justified it.
- Low blast-radius, frequently exercised actions graduate first.
- Heavy actions such as a full configuration restore stay permanently gated.

## How to verify

For every automatic action, produce the shadow-mode metrics and the probation record that justified promotion.
