# P-011 — Separate observation planes, single collection layer

## Statement

Signals are separated by nature — those contributing to a delivered service, and those describing the platform hosting it — but collected once and routed by attribute.

## Why

Separating audiences and retention needs is legitimate; instrumenting a source twice is not. Routing at the gateway keeps the split from becoming duplication.

## Implications

- One agent fleet, one gateway tier, several backends.
- Adding or replacing a backend is a gateway configuration change, not a redeployment.
- Cross-plane correlation is preserved by forwarding platform events into the service plane.

## How to verify

Check that no source sends to two backends and that a platform incident affecting a service is correlated automatically.
