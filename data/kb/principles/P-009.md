# P-009 — Independent failure domain

## Statement

The chain that restores service does not share cluster, storage or network path with the services it restores, and a break-glass procedure exists for its own unavailability.

## Why

The classic embarrassment of automated operations is the recovery tooling being unavailable during the incident it was built for.

## Implications

- Dedicated management cluster with its own storage and southbound paths.
- Critical fallback plans exported to a location reachable without the platform, refreshed by the same pipeline.
- At least two operators trained and periodically drilled.

## How to verify

Run the break-glass drill: apply a critical fallback plan with the management platform shut down.
