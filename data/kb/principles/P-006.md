# P-006 — One chain, several southbound methods

## Statement

A single orchestration, approval, audit and observability layer drives heterogeneous domains. Domain specificities are isolated in a connector layer.

## Why

The value of the chain is in its governance layer, not in its transport. Isolating transport keeps one audit trail and one approval model across domains that have nothing technically in common.

## Implications

- Adding a domain means adding a connector, not a parallel chain.
- The approval, logging and inventory model must not vary by domain.

## How to verify

Inspect the audit trail: an action on any domain must produce records of the same shape.
