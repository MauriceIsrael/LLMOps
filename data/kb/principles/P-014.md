# P-014 — The assistant is never on the critical path

## Statement

No closed loop, playbook, runbook or break-glass procedure may depend on the availability of a model or an assistant.

## Why

This is what makes the whole assistant layer optional, therefore deferrable, therefore low risk to commit to at bid stage.

## Implications

- The assistant layer can be switched off with no operational consequence beyond lost convenience.
- Knowledge surfaced by the assistant is captured in runbooks rather than left in conversations.

## How to verify

Disable the assistant and run a full remediation exercise; nothing should change except operator comfort.
