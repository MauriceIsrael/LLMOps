# P-012 — The assistant advises, it does not act

## Statement

Assistant tooling is read-only in production. It may explain, diagnose and prepare, but every state change continues through the approved workflow.

## Why

An assistant is a probabilistic component. Giving it a write path makes its failure mode operational instead of informational, and makes the audit trail ambiguous about who decided.

## Implications

- Custom tools expose read operations, plus at most a prepare operation returning a pre-filled workflow reference.
- Any write capability offered by a platform tool is disabled in the production profile.

## How to verify

Enumerate the tools exposed in production and confirm none can change state.
